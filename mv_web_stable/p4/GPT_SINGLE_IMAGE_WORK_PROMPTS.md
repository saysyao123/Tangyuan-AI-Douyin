# P4 · One-image-per-shot Work prompt

## Mandatory output format
Generate exactly **ONE** standalone **9:16 vertical film illustration PNG** in one image-generation call. No storyboard board, no multi-panel grid, no overview poster, no diagram, no table, no text, no timestamp, no captions or montage. If the result is a collage, reject it entirely and do NOT crop from it as a production substitute.

Global consistency: a single fictional Jiangnan woman, long black hair, charcoal traditional robe, muted coral-red scarf, rainy stone bridge at night, cold blue atmosphere and small amber lights. First use approved P3 L01 & L07 stills as character/style references. No automatic optical-flow human movement. Render captions separately in Canvas.

## L02 · 怎么想念没有
L02 solitary woman on moonlit rainy bridge at night, 3/4 back medium-long shot, feeling the empty place next to her. The red thread is gone; no flying photo; still posture and soft scarf motion. Empty space contributes to visual meaning.

L03 tabletop close shot in an intimate lamplit room, six torn fragments of the very same photograph, with clearly physical matching edges; pale blue rain at the window. No floating pieces, because the coded animation will join them. Also generate **one consistent photograph cutout** as a separate isolated prop in a later dedicated request.

L06 same bridge and woman world as P3 L01/L07, composition reserves open depth space at right for the single consistent photo prop, which the Skill will slow and float. Heroine looks toward that space; do not bake a differently drawn photograph into the image.

## L07 · action sequence
3 separate image calls with precisely the same scene/scale and character: shoulder-to-hand 3/4 view; (1) arm by body, (2) raised elbow / extended forearm, (3) wrist reaches just short of the floating photo. Preserve five fingers, sleeve length, head identity, body/foot plant.

## L08 · action sequence
3 separate image calls with the same portrait framing/appearance as original L08 still; (1) arm rising, (2) finger touches scarf, (3) hand rests against scarf. Review keyframe consistency before animating.

## Validation
Treat image provenance, 9:16 geometry, scene continuity, clear foreground/background separability, realistic anatomy and actual scene QA as separate gates. Commit an image only after reviewing it visually and recording its SHA256.
