- Each CUT serves lyric / beat / emotional punctuation.
- Use only established persistent assets: same female adventurer, same red/coral short cape outfit system, same small blue-white wind spirit, same highland lake world.

- Character locomotion must show readable foot lift → plant → push-off → center-of-gravity transfer → acceleration/deceleration; no sliding/drifting.
- Camera and environment move with the character, but camera motion must have a clear purpose and settle after peaks.
- Local close-ups must come from existing assets: eyes, hand, boot, cape, wind spirit, grass, lake reflection.
- Positive direction first; keep the world bright, cinematic, emotional, coherent, and animation-oriented.
