### S04｜如果还能一起回家
Decision: `REGENERATE / DIRECTOR-LOGIC PATCH`

- Failure occurs after foreground occlusion: the hidden section becomes an uncontrolled scene/pose reset and the second half loses understandable continuity.
- Important learning: full foreground occlusion is promising as an intentional transition mechanism from shot/scene A to shot/scene B, but is risky when asked to return to the same continuous scene without a separately defined post-occlusion target state.

### S05｜如果梦能模糊真假

- Rain/water appears on the wrong spatial side, as though inside the glass/interior volume.
- Surface ownership must be explicit before retrying.

### S06｜如果痛能随之融化
Decision: `FULL CONCEPT REBUILD`

- The entire performance/event design is visually unclear and physically awkward.
- Do not patch the existing action by adding more prose.
- Replace the shot concept from first principles while keeping the lyric function: pain should visibly change state / release / soften in a physically legible way.

### S07｜如果我们还是傻瓜
