## 1. User-locked direction

- **Single male protagonist is the absolute visual center.**
- Primary attraction goal: male-character attractiveness / restrained sexual tension.
- Sexual tension must come from **controlled approach, look-back, yielding distance, stable companionship/presence, eye-line and body-distance changes** — not oily posing, shirtless display, exaggerated seduction or fashion-commercial posturing.
- Overall visual feeling: **bright, healing, comfortable, breathable**, with strong female-viewer comfort rather than dark identity-horror.
- Priority remains: **lyric visual hit > light narrative continuity > camera spectacle**.
- Test route: **Face-Degrade -> Face-Completion**.
- Camera direction: actively test **moving character + camera–subject relationship**, choosing relationship before named camera move. Allowed relationship families include FOLLOW / LEAD / YIELD / OVERTAKE / DISCOVER when motivated.

## 2. Face-Degrade -> Face-Completion hard constraint

All first-frame images use **Face Degrade Level 3** as an input-adaptation layer only:

- hair information: 100%
- ears / jawline: ~85%
- face shape: ~75%
- brows: ~50%
- eyes: ~15–20%
- nose: ~20–25%
- mouth: ~15–20%
- skin texture: ~10–15%

The degradation applies only to facial-feature information. It must **not** reduce composition quality, lighting, body language, shot design, camera freedom, environment detail or overall image resolution.

Dynamic stage goal:
- facial identity/details complete and stabilize early enough for the intended performance beat;
- once face completion has occurred, the shot should not oscillate back into degraded/unstable facial structure;
- camera/subject design may continue through the completion transition instead of freezing the shot.

## 3. Shared world for A/B isolation

To isolate Director logic rather than art-direction roulette, both candidates use the same new world:

### Character
- one fictional East Asian man, late-20s appearance;
- lean, relaxed athletic silhouette; quiet confidence rather than model posing;
- soft black hair with natural wind movement;
- textured warm-white / pale-stone overshirt or shirt, relaxed collar, sleeves naturally loose/rolled; light stone trousers;
- no logos, no tattoos used as identity crutch, no jewelry that becomes a plot object;
- attractiveness comes from posture, movement, light, proximity, gaze and restraint.

### Space
A **bright coastal modernist retreat** that has not appeared in prior MV rounds:
- pale limestone / warm off-white architecture;
- long open-air colonnade and threshold openings;
- shallow reflecting-water edge in selected areas;
- sea horizon and wind-shaped pale dune grass beyond architecture;
- occasional translucent exterior linen/screen only where it has a Director function;
- late-afternoon clear light moving toward soft golden release, but never dark/night/noir.

### Palette / material
- warm ivory / pale limestone / sunlit skin-neutral warmth;
- clear sea blue-green used as environmental depth, not neon;
- restrained honey-gold sunlight near release;
- linen, stone, water, wind and natural skin/hair are the main tactile materials.
