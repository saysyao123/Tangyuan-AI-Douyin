## 1. Director Thesis

The MV turns letting-go into a changing camera relationship.

At first the camera receives and maintains intimate distance with the male protagonist. As the lyrics move through longing, restraint, abandoned expression, passing time and final release, the camera progressively gives him more passage and stops trying to restore closeness.

Final visual meaning:
**he is still worth looking at and still emotionally significant; release means the camera no longer needs to keep him close.**

## 2. Primary Visual Engine

`RELATIONAL DISTANCE / THRESHOLD PASSAGE`

World progression:
`bright colonnade -> limestone threshold -> open post-rain terrace -> sea horizon`.

Camera progression:
`LEAD -> HOLD -> YIELD -> FOLLOW/DISCOVER -> STOP PURSUING / WORLD OPEN`.

Auxiliary atmosphere enters only for NB04–NB05 and remains subordinate:
- post-rain surface evidence;
- one restrained wind passage;
- increasing horizon/negative space toward release.

## 3. Character / world continuity

Single fictional East Asian man, late-20s appearance; lean relaxed athletic silhouette; soft black hair; textured warm-white/pale-stone shirt/overshirt and light stone trousers.

World: bright coastal modernist retreat, pale limestone architecture, open colonnade, threshold openings, shallow water edge where justified, pale dune grass and sea horizon.

Palette: warm ivory / pale stone / natural skin warmth / restrained blue-green sea depth / mild honey-gold release.

No second human. No readable text. No phone/chat/letter storytelling. No dark/noir/cyber/ancient inheritance. No beauty-commercial posing.

## 4. Face-Degrade -> Face-Completion rule

First-frame input uses Face Degrade Level 3 only on facial feature information. Composition, lighting, silhouette, body language, wardrobe, environment and camera setup remain full quality.

Dynamic stage:
- face completes/stabilizes early;
- completed face does not oscillate back;
- camera and body movement continue naturally through completion;
- do not freeze the shot merely to protect face recovery.

## 5. Source S01｜APPROACH -> RESTRAINT

Natural Beat coverage: NB01 + NB02.
Lyric region: `有几次想你了 / 有几次忍住了`.
Final role: `HOOK + TENSION/HOLD`.

### Frame-0 / K0 target

- 9:16 bright medium shot in long pale-stone colonnade.
- Male protagonist already mid-walk, approximately mid-distance, body oriented toward camera travel axis.
- Camera is in front of him at a safe relational distance; frame contains enough architecture for backward movement.
- Face Degrade L3 only; hair/jaw/body/wardrobe and overall beauty remain intact.
- No foreground prop dependency; simple wind movement in hair/overshirt only.

### Primary action

`walk toward camera -> attraction/recognition becomes readable -> voluntarily stop at threshold`.

### Camera relationship

`LEAD -> HOLD`.

Motivation:
- `想你`: camera receives his approach; distance closes gently because subject speed slightly exceeds camera retreat.
- `忍住`: the camera stops when he stops; restraint is shown by removal of movement rather than a new flourish.

### Motion budget

- primary camera: backward LEAD tracking, mild amplitude;
- primary subject: stable forward walk -> stop;
- secondary physical motion: natural hair/garment wind.

### Required internal hits

- Face completion stabilizes early enough for readable eye-line during `想你`.
- No direct seduction pose; attraction comes from movement/proximity.
- Stop must be complete and legible during `忍住`.

### Clean endpoint

Man is stationary at threshold; camera also settled; only light fabric/hair residue continues.

### Avoid

- subject colliding with lens;
- exaggerated head turn;
- hand toward camera;
- orbit or crash push;
- abrupt stop with foot sliding.

## 6. Source S02｜ALMOST SPEAK -> CHOOSE PASSAGE

Natural Beat coverage: NB03.
Lyric region: `有几句想说的 / 都变成算了`.
Final role: `TURN`.

### Frame-0 / K0 target

- Medium-close to medium threshold composition; torso and breathing posture visible.
- Subject has just arrived in held state; not yet crossing.
- One translucent architectural linen/screen may occupy a controlled foreground/side plane.
- Camera has a clear diagonal escape/yield path.

### Primary action

`small inhale / eye-line lift / unfinished intention -> disengage gaze -> commit to passing through threshold`.

### Camera relationship

`HOLD -> YIELD`.

Motivation:
- `想说`: camera remains still enough to read breath and hesitation.
- `算了`: once subject chooses passage, camera yields diagonally to give him a physical route rather than pulling him back into intimacy.

### Optional linen element

Trigger: the semantic cancellation/passage after `算了`.
Function: physical foreground depth + motivated occlusion/transition possibility.
Range: S02 / threshold only.
Stop condition: linen is absent from terrace/coast sources.

### Motion budget

- primary camera: HOLD, then short diagonal YIELD;
- primary subject: incomplete outward intention -> pass;
- secondary physical motion: restrained linen/garment response.

### WHY CUT HERE

If a hidden cut is needed, use architectural/linen occlusion only after the passage is committed. The cut is motivated because both spatial zone and emotional relationship change.

### Clean endpoint

Subject has crossed the intimate threshold and established a stable outward travel direction.

### Avoid

- lip-sync burden;
- obvious “sad acting”;
- cloth covering the face for long periods;
- near-full occlusion before the semantic decision;
- linen following him into later scenes.

## 7. Source S03｜WEATHER HAS PASSED / TIME PASSES

Natural Beat coverage: NB04.
Lyric region: `有几场雨停了 / 有几阵风过了`.
Final role: `BRIDGE + EXPANSION`.

### Frame-0 / K0 target

- Rear/side medium-wide walking phase on open terrace.
- Wet limestone sheen / small residual water evidence already present; no active rainfall.
- Coast begins to become visible but does not yet dominate frame.
- Dune grass is available deeper in space for one readable wind event.

### Primary action

`continue walking through the world after weather has passed`.

### Camera relationship

`FOLLOW / side-rear DISCOVER`.

Motivation:
The lyric perspective shifts from internal choices to accumulated time. Camera movement exists to reveal the new open space and environmental after-state, not to showcase a tracking trick.

### Audiovisual handling

- `雨停`: show the after-state — wet stone/residual water — rather than generating a storm.
- `风过`: one clean passing gust moves grass/garment.
- Do not cut between the two lines; rain and wind are a parallel temporal pair.

### Motion budget

- primary camera: simple follow/side-rear travel;
- primary subject: stable walking direction;
- secondary physical system: one weather after-state/gust response.

### Clean endpoint

Open coast/horizon is now clearly established as the next emotional space.

### Avoid

- active rain starting/stopping on command;
- multiple gusts / storm spectacle;
- complex water splashes around feet;
- camera overtake if it destabilizes face/gait unnecessarily.

## 8. Source S04｜ATTACHMENT -> RELEASE

Natural Beat coverage: NB05 + final audio tail.
Lyric region: `有多舍不得，也该放下了`.
Final role: `PEAK + RELEASE`.

### Frame-0 / K0 target

- Medium to medium-wide front-three-quarter relation near sea-facing edge.
- Man remains visually significant and attractive; horizon is present but not yet dominant.
- Enough open space exists behind/beside him for distance to expand.
- Dune grass/garment edge can provide restrained physical residue.

### Primary action

`quiet attachment remains visible -> he chooses to continue into open space -> settles without asking the camera to restore closeness`.

### Camera relationship

`relational HOLD -> YIELD / STOP PURSUING -> WORLD OPEN`.

Motivation:
- `舍不得`: keep relational distance stable long enough for attachment to register.
- `放下`: subject turns/continues; camera does not chase to regain the close relation.
- `14.260–15.386s` tail: no new event; only open-frame breathing/release.

### Motion budget

- primary camera: restrained relational hold, then mild retreat/rise or stable open hold;
- primary subject: continue/settle toward horizon;
- secondary physical motion: grass/garment wind residue.

### Required emotional rule

Release is not disappearance, rejection or emotional numbness. He remains present in the final composition while the world receives more frame area.

### Clean endpoint

Stable open composition with man still present and negative space available through final tail.

### Avoid

- camera suddenly pushing back toward his face after `放下`;
- protagonist disappearing into fog/light;
- dramatic running;
- cliff-edge stunt;
- orbit;
- extreme crane amplitude.

## 9. Whole-set differentiation

Shot scale:
`moving medium -> threshold medium/medium-close -> travel medium-wide -> open medium-wide/wide`.

Camera relation:
`LEAD -> HOLD/YIELD -> FOLLOW/DISCOVER -> STOP PURSUING/WORLD OPEN`.

Dominant event:
`approach/stop -> almost express/pass -> travel through after-weather world -> release distance`.

Environment:
`colonnade -> threshold -> terrace -> coast`.

No two sources should collapse into the same thumbnail-level role.

## 10. First-frame handoff / Creative Drift QA

For every K0:
- preserve source-specific spatial zone;
- preserve intended camera–subject relationship at frame 0;
- preserve body travel/stop phase;
- maintain single-character policy and same wardrobe/world;
- include only the source’s allowed optional elements;
- leave sufficient camera/action space for the planned movement.

First-frame generation may improve framing/beauty but may not replace the semantic task with a different pose, scene or prop story.

Once HG03 accepts actual K0 pixels, those pixels become authority over this prose. Dynamic prompts must continue the accepted phase rather than resetting it.
