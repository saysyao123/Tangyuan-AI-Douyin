## Director Thesis

At first the camera keeps meeting and holding the man in intimate distance; as the lyrics move from longing to restraint to release, the camera progressively stops holding him and finally allows distance, weather and horizon to pass between them.

Primary Visual Engine:
`RELATIONAL DISTANCE / THRESHOLD PASSAGE`.

Camera progression:
`LEAD -> HOLD -> YIELD -> FOLLOW/DISCOVER -> STOP PURSUING / WORLD OPEN`.

## WHY CUT HERE

- NB01 -> NB02: no mandatory cut; the approach-to-stop should read as one continuous semantic action inside S01.
- S01 -> S02: cut only after the physical stop/held state has completed and the lyric intention changes from restraint to expression.
- S02 -> S03: preferred motivated handoff is threshold/linen/architectural occlusion after `算了`; space and relationship both change here.
- Inside NB04: do **not** cut between `雨停` and `风过`; their parallel temporal structure is stronger in one continuous source.
- S03 -> S04: cut when the open terrace has fully revealed the coast and the lyric moves from elapsed time to direct admission/release.
- Inside NB05: do **not** cut between `舍不得` and `放下`; the emotional meaning requires attachment and release to exist in one sustained relational arc.

## Optional-element bounds

- Translucent linen/screen: NB03 threshold only; stop after the passage transition.
- Residual post-rain water: NB04 terrace only; no active storm.
- One readable gust: NB04 only / restrained; do not escalate wind into spectacle.
- No optional element may override the single male protagonist or relational-distance engine.

- S03: after-rain / wind time-passage travel;
- S04: attachment visible -> camera stops pursuing -> open release.
