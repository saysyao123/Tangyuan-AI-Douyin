## 1. Revision reason

HG04 picture-preview review rejected the previous four-source / mostly single-shot design.
The failure was not image beauty or camera smoothness. The failure was **insufficient lyric-visible coverage**:

- 7 locked lyric lines were compressed into 5 Natural Beats and then into only 4 main visual source blocks;
- the OSS relational-distance thesis survived, but it became too conceptual;
- several lyric verbs were represented only by generic movement (`walk / stop / walk away`) instead of a lyric-specific visible event;
- the edit had too few independent source atoms, so picture rhythm could not land all seven lyric meanings cleanly.

Correction:

`R3 direct lyric visual answer` is restored as priority 1.
`OSS camera motive / relational progression / WHY CUT HERE` becomes the continuity layer, not the replacement for lyric-hit visuals.

## 2. New hard Director rule

For this 15.386s excerpt:

- every one of the 7 lyric lines must have at least **one independently readable visual anchor**;
- a visual anchor does not require a hard cut exactly at lyric start;
- production must create **more edit atoms than final edit needs**;
- target source pool: **5 new ~5s raw dynamic sources**, normally 2 shots each; one task-specific 3-shot source is allowed for `雨停 -> 风过`;
- target normalized pool: **9–11 usable ATOM candidates** before picture edit;
- expected final edit: approximately **7–9 selected visible shots/cuts**, chosen by lyric truth + action integrity, not equal duration;
- the four previously generated beautiful relational clips may remain as supplemental fallback material after fresh QA, but they are not sufficient as the new primary coverage plan.

The previous OSS insight is retained, but subordinated to lyric-hit visuals:

`camera receives him`
-> `he physically restrains the impulse`
-> `camera waits for words that never come`
-> `camera yields when he gives up saying them`
-> `camera discovers the world after rain and wind`
-> `his hand finally releases`
-> `camera stops pursuing`.

This gives both:
1. direct lyric readability;
2. one continuous camera-subject emotional progression.
