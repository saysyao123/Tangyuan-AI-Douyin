## 1. Director Thesis

**The song becomes visible through one changing relationship: at first the camera keeps meeting and holding the man in intimate distance; as the lyrics move from longing to restraint to release, the camera progressively stops holding him and finally allows distance, weather and horizon to pass between them.**

The emotional subject is not “forgetting someone.”
It is **choosing not to keep closing the distance** even though attachment remains.

This makes male attractiveness serve the lyric: attraction is created through controlled proximity, gaze, approach and yielding space; the final release gains meaning because the camera itself changes behavior.

## 2. Primary Visual Engine

### Primary engine: `RELATIONAL DISTANCE / THRESHOLD PASSAGE`

One visual law governs the whole MV:

`camera meets him -> camera and subject stop together -> camera yields passage -> camera observes time/weather passing -> camera stops pursuing and lets space open`.

Spatial progression:
`colonnade -> threshold -> open post-rain terrace -> sea horizon`.

The protagonist crosses increasingly open thresholds; the camera–subject relationship changes with each semantic decision.

### Auxiliary engine: `ATMOSPHERIC AFTER-STATE`

Used only after the lyric shifts from internal conflict to time passing:
- wet stone / draining residual water for `雨停`;
- one readable gust through grass/garment for `风过`;
- clear horizon / warm air for final release.

This auxiliary engine enters at NB04 and remains subordinate to the protagonist/camera relation.

### Explicitly rejected engines

- No second-person narrative reenactment: it would weaken single-male visual focus and add identity instability.
- No literal chat/letter/text concept: too explanatory and visually generic.
- No magical weather transformation: unnecessary generation load and weakens healing realism.

## 3. Audiovisual structure by Natural Beat

### NB01｜0.000–1.880｜`有几次想你了`

Primary audiovisual relationship: `PARALLEL + phrase-level SYNC`.

- Sound fact: longing surfaces immediately.
- Viewer experience: attraction/recognition should become readable without melodrama.
- Subject intention: approach, but not perform seduction.
- Camera motivation: **emotion + relational** — camera retreats while receiving his approach, allowing distance to close slightly.
- Camera relationship: `LEAD`.
- Start: medium distance, man already walking naturally.
- Process: subject speed is marginally greater than camera retreat, so relation becomes gently more intimate.
- End/stop reason: motion settles as the phrase completes; do not crash into portrait distance.
- Key hit: eye-line becomes more legible inside the shot on `想你`, not via a cut.

WHY CUT HERE: **do not cut inside NB01**. The approach is the semantic action; interrupting it would weaken attraction and continuity.

### NB02｜2.020–3.640｜`有几次忍住了`

Primary audiovisual relationship: `NEGATIVE SPACE / RESTRAINT`.

- Sound fact: the active verb changes from feeling to restraint.
- Subject intention: stop the approach before fully crossing the threshold.
- Camera motivation: **emotional stop** — the camera also stops instead of adding another flourish.
- Camera relationship: `LEAD -> HOLD`.
- Start: continuation of S01 approach.
- Process: body reaches the threshold and stops; only wind/cloth residue continues.
- End/stop reason: restraint must remain visible for a short hold rather than instantly converting into the next action.

WHY CUT HERE: if S01 ends here, cut only **after** the completed stop/hold, because the semantic state has changed from movement to held intention.

### NB03｜3.780–7.180｜`有几句想说的 -> 都变成算了`

Primary audiovisual relationship: `SYNC -> RELEASE BRIDGE`.

- Sound fact: an intended expression forms and is then cancelled.
- Subject intention: almost turn toward the intimate camera relation, then choose passage instead of expression.
- Camera motivation: **performance first, then action**.
- Camera relationship: `HOLD -> YIELD`.
- Start: closer threshold relation, torso and breath readable.
- Process:
  1. `想说`: small inhale / eye-line lift / unfinished forward intention; no exaggerated mouth acting.
  2. `算了`: gaze disengages; subject commits to crossing past the threshold.
  3. Camera performs a short diagonal YIELD to give him a physical path.
- End/stop reason: passage is complete; intimate frontal relationship has been broken.

Optional element: translucent architectural linen/screen.
- Trigger: `算了` passage needs a physically motivated transition, not decorative softness.
- Function: foreground depth + partial/near-full occlusion as subject crosses.
- Range: **NB03 only / threshold only**.
- Peak use: the actual crossing after `算了`.
- Stop condition: once the subject exits the threshold zone, linen never follows him to terrace/coast.

WHY CUT HERE: use the architectural/linen occlusion near the end of passage as a motivated hidden cut into NB04. The cut exists because the relationship and space have changed, not because a new lyric line began.

### NB04｜7.320–10.700｜`有几场雨停了 -> 有几阵风过了`

Primary audiovisual relationship: `PARALLEL / ATMOSPHERIC BRIDGE`.

- Sound fact: lyric perspective shifts from internal decisions to accumulated passing time.
- Subject intention: continue forward without looking back for reassurance.
- Camera motivation: **information + spatial expansion** — reveal the world after the threshold and let weather after-states become visible.
- Camera relationship: `FOLLOW / side-rear DISCOVER`.
- Start: man already in stable travel direction on open terrace.
- Process:
  - `雨停`: wet limestone / residual water after-state is visible; no active rain event required.
  - `风过`: one clean gust travels through dune grass and garment; protagonist movement remains simple.
- End/stop reason: camera has revealed the open coast as the next emotional space.

Optional elements:
1. residual water
   - trigger: `雨停`;
   - function: prove rain has passed without adding a storm;
   - range: NB04 terrace only;
   - stop: no water-event emphasis in final release.
2. one readable wind gust
   - trigger: `风过`;
   - function: make passage visible physically;
   - range: second half NB04 into early handoff only;
   - stop: do not keep escalating wind into a spectacle.

WHY CUT HERE: cut from NB03 occlusion into the new open terrace because the lyric perspective changes from inner choice to time/weather. Do not cut between `雨停` and `风过`; their parallel grammar is stronger as one continuous source.

### NB05｜10.860–15.386｜`有多舍不得，也该放下了` + tail

Primary audiovisual relationship: `PARALLEL -> phrase SYNC -> RELEASE / NEGATIVE SPACE`.

- Sound fact: attachment is admitted and release is chosen in the same sentence.
- Viewer experience: the man remains desirable/meaningful; release should not visually punish or erase him.
- Subject intention: allow attachment to be seen, then continue into open space without asking the camera to follow.
- Camera motivation: **relational resolution**.
- Camera relationship: `front/three-quarter HOLD -> YIELD / WORLD-OPENING RELEASE`.
- Start: camera is close enough to read quiet attachment; no beauty-ad close-up.
- `舍不得`: hold relational distance and eye-line/body tension long enough to register.
- `放下`: subject turns/continues toward open horizon; camera does **not** chase to restore intimacy.
- After `14.260s`: no new narrative event; slow retreat/mild rise or stable open hold may continue through the `15.386s` tail.
- End/stop reason: the frame reaches a stable open composition with enough negative space to breathe.

WHY CUT HERE: do not cut after `舍不得` merely because the clause turns. The meaning comes from seeing attachment and release in one sustained relational arc. This should be the longest/cleanest final source block.

## 4. Production-source allocation

Use **4 raw ~5s sources**, not one-per-lyric and not H3 10–15s containers.

| Source | Natural Beat coverage | Camera relation | Primary event | Source grammar | Final role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B-S01 | NB01 + NB02 | LEAD -> HOLD | approach -> voluntary stop | 1-shot | HOOK / TENSION |
| B-S02 | NB03 | HOLD -> YIELD | almost speak -> choose passage | 1-shot, optional motivated occlusion | TURN |
| B-S03 | NB04 | FOLLOW / DISCOVER | travel through after-rain/wind world | 1-shot | BRIDGE / EXPANSION |
| B-S04 | NB05 | HOLD -> YIELD / world-open | attachment visible -> camera stops pursuing | 1-shot | PEAK / RELEASE |

No default multi-shot source is required. Internal cut is allowed only if the NB03 occlusion cannot be represented cleanly as one continuous camera passage.

## 5. Camera / subject / space progression

The camera relationships form one authored progression rather than a menu of moves:

`LEAD (meet him)`
-> `HOLD (restraint)`
-> `YIELD (let him pass)`
-> `FOLLOW/DISCOVER (witness time passing)`
-> `STOP PURSUING / OPEN WORLD (release)`.

This progression is the primary visual engine and must survive first-frame and dynamic-prompt handoff.

## 6. Generation-load discipline

Each source obeys:
`1 primary camera relationship + 1 primary subject action + 1 secondary physical system`.

- B-S01: LEAD/HOLD + walk/stop + garment wind.
- B-S02: HOLD/YIELD + inhale/pass + threshold linen.
- B-S03: FOLLOW/DISCOVER + stable walk + after-rain/wind evidence.
- B-S04: relational HOLD/YIELD + continue/release + grass/garment residue.

Avoid hand-object interaction, mirror, readable text, fluid-hand-face stacks, or large head rotations.

## 7. Creative Drift QA handoff

### Director -> First Frame immutables
For every source, K0 must preserve:
- the intended camera–subject relationship at frame 0;
- body travel direction / stop phase;
- spatial zone in the coastal retreat;
- wardrobe/world continuity;
- only the optional elements explicitly allowed for that source;
- enough body/environment information for the planned movement.

First-frame generation may improve composition/beauty but must not replace the source’s semantic task with a different pose or scene.

### First Frame -> Dynamic Prompt immutables
Accepted actual K0 pixels become authority.
Dynamic prompt must continue the accepted K0 phase rather than resetting the subject/camera merely to recreate this prose.

### Drift failures
Block or patch if downstream introduces:
- second human;
- unrelated prop symbolism;
- linen outside NB03 threshold;
- active storm instead of post-rain evidence;
- unmotivated orbit/whip/zoom;
- final camera re-chasing the man after `放下`;
- camera movement that looks impressive but reverses the relational progression.
