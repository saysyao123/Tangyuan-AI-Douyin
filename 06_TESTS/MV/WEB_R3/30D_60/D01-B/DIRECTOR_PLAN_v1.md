# D01-B｜First-frame Director Plan v1

Status: `DIRECTOR_PLAN_LOCKED / FIRST-FRAME PRODUCTION AUTHORIZED`
Song: `我救自己于人间水火`
Lane: `S / Stable-Fast`
Format: `9:16 vertical / four separate images / no collage`

## 1. Director thesis

The protagonist is not waiting to be rescued. She restores a damaged life-supporting place with her own hands.

Visual sentence:

`A sealed alpine seed conservatory begins to breathe again because she opens it, cares for herself inside it, uncovers one surviving flower, and continues upward into the larger world.`

The set must feel healing because agency produces clarity, not because fantasy effects erase pain.

## 2. Independent world lock

### Location

A fictional high-altitude seed conservatory on an open mountain plateau:
- long oxidized-steel greenhouse ribs;
- weathered translucent glass, some panes clouded or safely missing;
- charcoal planting soil and a few blackened timber edges from old damage;
- shallow geometric irrigation channels;
- exterior maintenance stairs and a broad ridge beyond.

It is neither ancient nor futuristic. It is a real working place after damage, not a ruin-tourist spectacle.

### Palette

- chalk-white glass and mineral light;
- clear highland cyan sky;
- oxidized rust-red steel;
- charcoal soil;
- restrained new-leaf green;
- small white flower as the cleanest highlight.

Avoid the previous MV's rain-night blue, warm amber interiors, charcoal veil, domestic corridor and dawn-city grammar.

### Light

Early clear morning after bad weather has passed:
- hard-but-gentle high-altitude daylight;
- crisp air and visible fine dust only when backlit;
- no orange fantasy sunrise;
- no neon, magical particles or artificial bloom effects.

## 3. Character lock

Same fictional young adult East Asian woman in all four frames:
- late 20s to early 30s;
- lean practical build, natural face, no celebrity resemblance;
- dark hair gathered in a low practical knot, a few wind-loosened strands;
- pale mineral-gray canvas work jacket with subtle rust-colored repair stitching;
- cream inner shirt;
- muted terracotta work trousers;
- weathered brown work boots;
- bare face, no veil, no mask, no jewelry, no beauty-commercial makeup.

Performance language:
- capable, tired but not collapsed;
- emotion carried by posture, hands, breath and task focus;
- no crying pose, no looking into camera for sympathy;
- no second human in any frame or later dynamic source.

## 4. Four first frames

### S01｜0.000–4.540｜Open the trapped system

Role: `HOOK / SELF-RESCUE`

Composition:
- extreme wide vertical interior down the central greenhouse aisle;
- environment occupies at least 75% of frame;
- converging rust-red ribs rise toward a jammed roof vent;
- shallow irrigation channels and charcoal beds create strong depth lines;
- the woman is lower-middle frame, full body but relatively small.

0-second dynamic anchor:
- she already has both hands on a long manual vent lever;
- knees and shoulders show controlled strain;
- the roof vent is halfway open, not closed and not finished;
- one blade of clear white daylight enters diagonally through the opening;
- fine dust and a thin veil of cool condensation are already moving toward the new airflow;
- jacket hem and loose hair strands indicate direction.

Must read instantly:
`she is opening her own way out`.

Do not show:
- active flames, flood, rescue workers, dramatic collapse, giant smoke cloud.

Dynamic potential:
- lever completes a short pull;
- vent opens another small amount;
- airflow carries dust upward;
- camera can use a very mild forward drift only.

### S02｜4.540–8.420｜Practical self-care

Role: `HOLD / SELF-LOVE`

Composition:
- high three-quarter medium-full shot beside a square stone irrigation cistern;
- woman kneels or sits steadily on one heel, body fully readable;
- clean water and her hands are the primary foreground event;
- glass roof and rust frame remain recognizable but softer in depth;
- not a face close-up.

0-second dynamic anchor:
- both hands already cup clear water just above the cistern surface, beginning to lift toward her face;
- concentric ripples spread from where her hands left the water;
- jacket sleeves are pushed back, forearms clean and uninjured;
- a folded clean cloth and metal cup are visible beside her from frame 0;
- her breathing posture has softened compared with S01.

Must read instantly:
`self-love means keeping herself alive and cared for`.

Do not show:
- wounds, bandages, blood, makeup mirror, spa luxury, glamour posing.

Dynamic potential:
- water lifts and drips naturally;
- she washes her face or drinks in one simple motion;
- camera remains locked or performs a tiny lateral drift.

### S03｜8.420–12.360｜Remember the one flower

Role: `TURN / BLOOM PEAK`

Composition:
- low near-ground perspective;
- one small white alpine flower in sharp foreground, already fully existing and already blooming;
- dark planting soil and a soot-dark protective canvas create tactile contrast;
- the woman appears half-to-full figure in middle background, not another close portrait;
- depth separates flower, fabric and woman clearly.

0-second dynamic anchor:
- she is already pulling the edge of the protective canvas back with one hand;
- the cover is halfway lifted, forming a diagonal dark plane;
- a narrow strip of real morning light has just reached the flower;
- the flower bends slightly in the first entering breeze;
- her other hand hovers near the soil but does not touch or manipulate the flower.

Must read instantly:
`she remembers life because one flower is still here`.

Do not show:
- magical instant growth, glowing petals, many flowers, butterflies, fairy particles, sentimental tears.

Dynamic potential:
- canvas pulls back a little farther;
- natural light expands across the soil;
- flower and a few leaves respond to airflow;
- focus can move subtly from fabric edge to flower.

### S04｜12.360–15.987｜Continue seeking

Role: `RELEASE / ASCENT`

Composition:
- extreme wide exterior vertical landscape;
- conservatory occupies lower third, mountain plateau and layered sky dominate upper frame;
- broad oxidized maintenance stair climbs along the greenhouse exterior;
- the same woman is midway upward, small but unmistakable;
- one lower stair flight descends behind her while the higher flight continues toward the roof ridge, making up/down spatially legible.

0-second dynamic anchor:
- one boot is planted on a higher step;
- one hand already reaches for the next rail section;
- body weight is transferring upward;
- wind moves jacket back panel, loose hair and sparse highland grass in one coherent direction;
- the horizon is visible but not yet fully revealed behind a greenhouse rib.

Must read instantly:
`the search continues beyond the repaired shelter`.

Do not show:
- cliff danger, running, jumping, flying, heroic superhero pose, giant sun disc.

Dynamic potential:
- she climbs two or three controlled steps;
- a slow crane-retreat can reveal more plateau and sky;
- primary action lands by `15.560s`, then the image holds the open horizon through the fade tail.

## 5. Whole-set differentiation

| Frame | Scale | View | Dominant material | Main action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | extreme wide | central architectural depth | steel / glass / air | pull vent open |
| S02 | medium-full | high three-quarter | clean water / stone | lift water to self |
| S03 | low detail-to-depth | flower foreground | soil / canvas / petal | uncover flower |
| S04 | extreme wide | exterior vertical landscape | stair / grass / sky | climb upward |

No two consecutive frames share the same scale, camera axis, body pose or dominant material.

## 6. Stable-Fast generation constraints

- cinematic photorealism, not illustration or game art;
- physical materials and natural light over spectacle;
- one difficult event maximum per frame;
- all narratively important objects must exist in frame 0;
- only one human, already present in every human source;
- hands visible but tasks mechanically simple;
- no readable text, logos, advertising, watermark or modern brand;
- no extra people, animals or vehicles;
- no ancient costume, veil, mask, hairpin, rain window, subway, warm-lamp corridor or prior-song prop inheritance;
- no collage, contact sheet, storyboard panel or embedded labels.

2. S02 reads as practical self-care rather than beauty content;
3. S03 has one unmistakable flower and no magical growth;
4. S04 provides genuine landscape release and vertical seeking;
5. the same woman and wardrobe remain continuous;
6. all four frames are separately usable 0-second dynamic anchors;
7. image quality is healing, clean and real without plastic AI gloss.
