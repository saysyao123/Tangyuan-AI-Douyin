## 0. Why v1 failed

The v1 greenhouse solution translated self-rescue into functional repair work. It was semantically defensible but aesthetically weak for this MV:
- industrial greenhouse ribs + mechanical lever created utilitarian labor imagery rather than poetic self-rescue;
- the world had no strong beauty motif or memorable visual symbol;
- the newly selected ancient-style pearl-glow heroine, veil and noble wardrobe conflicted with the practical-workwear greenhouse world;
- S01 composition became a symmetric industrial hall with a centered figure, producing scale without elegance;
- gray/rust materials dominated, so makeup and costume had no coherent relationship with environment;
- the action was visually literal but emotionally generic.

Therefore v1 visual world, greenhouse, lever, cistern, canvas cover and maintenance stairs are rejected. Natural Beat and audio timeline remain valid.

## 1. New director thesis

This film is not about repairing a damaged facility. It is about a woman **walking herself out of the residue of water and fire**, gently reclaiming herself, recognizing that life still blooms, and continuing upward without waiting for rescue.

Visual sentence:

`water-and-fire aftermath -> self-possession -> one iris still blooming -> open ascent`

Emotional priority:
`lyric visual hit > light narrative continuity > spectacle`

Beauty principle:
Every frame must first work as an elegant standalone cinematic still, then as a 0-second performance anchor.

## 2. New independent world lock｜白石鸢尾水庭

A fictional Eastern high-mountain sanctuary built from pale limestone terraces, shallow mirror-water courts, thin silver-gray screens, dark bronze fittings and restrained iris gardens. It references no specific dynasty, film or IP.

Recurring visual motifs:
- water: mirror surface / rinsing / reflection / breath;
- fire: only residual ember glow or scorched material, never active disaster spectacle;
- iris: one white or very pale lilac iris as the life motif;
- stair / ridge: unfinished seeking and vertical release;
- pearl gauze: links heroine makeup, veil, costume and environment.

Palette:
- pearl white limestone;
- mist lilac / silver-gray-blue;
- cool jade-gray water;
- dark bronze / charcoal traces;
- tiny restrained ember-rust accent only in S01;
- clear highland cyan in S04.

Light progression:
`cool residual side light -> soft pearl daylight -> luminous natural flower light -> open clean highland sky`

Forbidden inheritance:
- no greenhouse or industrial architecture;
- no rain-night window grammar;
- no subway / station / urban signage;
- no previous-song blue-amber corridor world;
- no giant fantasy portal, magic particles or glowing supernatural flower.

## 3. Heroine lock｜Iris Misty Silver Pearl Glow + Veil

Same fictional adult East Asian woman in all four frames, visual age about 24–28, no celebrity resemblance.

Face:
- gentle oval face, naturally full forehead and mid-face;
- long clear eyes with naturally extended outer corners;
- refined smooth nose bridge;
- calm, restrained expression; never pleading or melodramatic.

Makeup:
- sheer cool-white translucent base preserving realistic skin texture and slight natural flush;
- misty purple + light gray-silver + pale pink fine shimmer gradient around upper eyelid;
- fine pearl light at inner eye and under-eye area;
- low-saturation cool pink blush across mid-face and lightly below eyes;
- misty cool pink-bean-paste lip color exists under veil but is not a visual focus.

Hair/accessories:
- jet-black hair in a slightly high, full, smooth updo;
- one primary silver-white iris hairpin;
- restrained transparent pearl drops and one mother-of-pearl comb;
- avoid excessive accessory count that causes identity drift.

Veil｜HARD:
- thin translucent pearl-gray-lilac gauze;
- continuously covers nose, mouth and lower face in every frame;
- never removed, lifted or made opaque;
- eye performance is the main facial acting layer.

Wardrobe｜HARD:
- misty purple-gray intersecting-collar long robe;
- silver-gray-blue pleated lower layer;
- pearl-white light-gauze half-sleeve outer layer;
- delicate embroidery and restrained silver-pearl accents;
- modest neckline, elegant rather than ornate costume-showpiece.

Performance:
- self-directed, calm, physically capable;
- emotion through eyes, hands, posture and breath;
- no crying, helpless collapse, glamour posing or looking to camera for sympathy.

## 4. Four revised first frames

### S01｜0.000–4.540｜走出水火残境
Role: `HOOK / SELF-RESCUE`

Composition:
- 35mm cinematic medium-wide, slightly low eye level; not an extreme wide;
- heroine occupies roughly 30–35% of frame height so makeup/veil remain readable;
- a pale-limestone circular gate and shallow mirror-water court create layered depth rather than industrial symmetry;
- camera is offset from center for a more elegant asymmetrical composition.

0-second dynamic anchor:
- heroine is already halfway through a shallow reflective water threshold;
- one hand presses open a soot-dark iris-patterned bronze lattice door that is already partly open;
- the other hand lightly gathers only enough robe fabric to step safely, not a fashion pose;
- behind her, far and low in frame, one narrow bronze ember channel leaves only a restrained residual warm reflection in water;
- ahead of her is cool pearl dawn, not golden fantasy light;
- veil edge, gauze sleeve and water ripples move coherently with a cross-breeze.

Must read:
`她没有等人来救，她自己穿过了水火之后的门。`

Beauty target:
pearl skin + mist-lilac veil against pale stone and dark reflective water; one tiny ember accent only.

### S02｜4.540–8.420｜把自己重新照顾好
Role: `HOLD / SELF-LOVE`

Composition:
- intimate medium shot / three-quarter profile at a white-stone water pavilion;
- shallow oval jade-gray basin occupies foreground; cool white iris shadows fall across stone;
- heroine upper body and hands readable; not a beauty-ad close-up.

0-second dynamic anchor:
- both hands are already in the act of slowly rinsing soot from one hand in clear water; no wounds, no blood;
- a few droplets fall from fingers and create soft rings;
- shoulders are relaxed compared with S01;
- her eyes look down toward her own hands, not at a mirror and not at camera;
- veil stays fully in place; pearl hair ornaments catch only tiny highlights.

Must read:
`自爱不是一句话，是先把自己照顾回来。`

Beauty target:
clear skin, iris makeup and translucent veil reflected softly in water without becoming a vanity portrait.

### S03｜8.420–12.360｜记得花开
Role: `TURN / BLOOM PEAK`

Composition:
- low 50mm detail-to-medium composition;
- a single already-blooming white iris grows from a crack between pale stone and charcoal soil in sharp foreground;
- heroine appears in middle depth, large enough to identify but secondary to flower;
- a blackened fallen branch creates one dark diagonal, not a canvas sheet.

0-second dynamic anchor:
- heroine has one hand already lifting the light end of the fallen branch away from the iris;
- the iris is untouched and already blooming;
- natural light reaches the flower petal edge and one side of her eyes at the same moment;
- a faint breeze moves petal, veil and gauze sleeve in one direction.

Must read:
`苦难之后，她仍看见并保护那一朵花。`

Beauty target:
white iris, charcoal trace, pearl-lilac heroine, precise shallow depth of field; no magic glow.

### S04｜12.360–15.987｜上下求索
Role: `RELEASE / ASCENT`

Composition:
- extreme-wide highland exterior, but heroine silhouette remains recognizable;
- long pale-stone stair climbs diagonally along a mountain water terrace toward an upper ridge;
- lower terrace and upper path are both visible, making vertical seeking legible;
- cloud sea is below the ridge, not filling the frame with fantasy fog.

0-second dynamic anchor:
- heroine is midway on the stair, one foot already transferring upward to the next step;
- one hand reaches for a slim dark-bronze rail while the other remains free;
- long pearl-white gauze layer and veil tail move backward in the highland wind;
- the highest ridge is visible but not yet reached;
- clean cyan sky opens above, while pale stone and a few restrained irises connect to previous frames.

Must read:
`她继续向上，也仍然允许答案没有结束。`

Beauty target:
large negative space, elegant diagonal ascent, clean horizon, small but graceful heroine; no superhero pose.

## 6. Set-level beauty QA｜HARD

A frame fails even if semantically correct when any of the following occurs:
- location feels utilitarian, abandoned-industrial or like a game level;
- costume and environment belong to different aesthetic worlds;
- gray/rust dominates without pearl/lilac/highland color hierarchy;
- heroine is centered mechanically with no foreground/midground/background layering;
- action reads as manual labor rather than lyrical agency;
- makeup is invisible in every frame;
- accessories become costume clutter;
- image is merely 'epic' but not elegant;
- scene could be swapped into another lyric without losing meaning.

The whole set must pass:
`beautiful standalone still + lyric-specific semantic hit + performable 0-second anchor + four-frame visual progression`.
