## 1. Whole-film proposition

这不是“被甩后难过”的普通人物写真，而是一条**外部盛夏不变、内部世界逐步失温**的短 MV。

一个虚构的东方成年男性独自在临近闭馆的现代主义室内游泳馆里活动。水、玻璃、镜面本来都能映出他；随着歌词推进，倒影先被切开、扰散、错位，灯再一盏盏离开他，最终他亲手覆盖镜面，让“当初真的自己”从画面里消失。

核心优先级：
`歌词视觉命中 > 独立画面美感 > 轻叙事连续 > 炫技镜头`

## 2. World Bible

### Space
同一座有明确空间连续性的城市室内游泳馆：
- 更衣室镜面与成排储物柜；
- 主泳池与红色泳道线；
- 池畔不锈钢扶手 / 玻璃隔断；
- 长更衣通道；
- 主池大厅远端的玻璃砖采光墙。

### Time / light
- 时间：盛夏傍晚、闭馆前。
- 外部日光：始终暖金、偏白、强而真实。
- 内部人工光：从中性逐步转冷并减少。
- 不是“天气突然变冬天”；寒冬只通过光色、空间空度、湿冷材质和人物状态表达。

### Palette / materials
- warm summer: amber glass brick / sun-white highlights;
- pool: pale aqua / clear cyan;
- structural neutral: off-white / brushed steel / charcoal;
- one controlled red accent: lane rope only;
- late interior cold: restrained steel-cyan, not neon.

### Protagonist
- one fictional East Asian adult male only;
- attractive, restrained, slightly damp short dark hair;
- natural skin, no celebrity likeness;
- off-white thin summer knit/shirt + charcoal loose trousers;
- no costume changes, jewelry hero props or fashion-logo styling;
- performance is contained: gaze, breathing, hand pressure, gait and pauses do more than exaggerated crying.

## 3. Source architecture

Produce **6 independent 9:16 first frames / 6 raw video sources** as the default source plan.

Each source is designed around approximately 5s-class generation stability, but is not a final 5s edit block. Internal clean atoms/arcs may later be trimmed and recombined.

### P01｜Repair / Empty
**Lyric responsibility:** `爱总是 好了疤 忘了痛 / 让人脑袋空空`

- First-frame scale: intimate medium, mirror composition.
- K0 primary event: transparent waterproof repair film is already lifted at one corner from a thin crack in the locker-room mirror; his fingertips are holding that lifted edge.
- Static base: empty lockers repeat deep behind the reflected protagonist.
- Main action entrance: he slowly peels the film away; no glass breaks.
- Camera: initially HOLD, then a very mild controlled dolly-in.
- Physical residue: damp mirror edge / tiny condensation, no magical particles.
- Settled possibility: film leaves frame; mirror remains cracked and empty room becomes more legible.
- Beauty reason: warm glass-brick summer light cuts across a cool empty locker room, giving the face/reflection a quiet split rather than a beauty-push portrait.
- Avoid: blood, bandage on skin, broken glass falling, extra reflections that look like another person.

### P02｜Red under the reflection / Hurry
**Lyric responsibility:** `直到心 破了洞 见了红 / 换来步履匆匆`

- First-frame scale: high overhead wide, pool geometry dominant.
- K0 primary event: protagonist is seen both physically near pool edge and as a coherent water reflection; a small ripple has just begun at the reflected chest area; a deep-red lane rope is visible beneath the water but not yet fully centered in the ripple.
- Action entrance: ripple expands and reveals/cuts across the red lane; subject turns and begins a fast purposeful walk along the deck.
- Camera: overhead reveal can transition into short side-follow movement; no wild crane.
- Residue: wet footprints / fading ripple.
- Beauty reason: strict aqua tile geometry + one red line gives “见了红” a sharp visual hit without injury literalism.
- Avoid: red liquid, wounds, falling, swimming, complex splash physics.

### P03｜Only one / Let go
**Lyric responsibility:** `从开始情有独钟 / 到最后泪眼汹涌 / 承诺全被风吹得无影无踪`

- First-frame scale: medium side profile with depth through pool glass.
- K0 primary event: one thin unbranded white wristband/tie is already tied once around a stainless rail and stretched lightly toward his fingers; he is touching/holding the free end, not tying a knot from scratch.
- Action entrance: he presses it for one restrained beat; ventilation wind pulls harder; fingers open and the strip leaves.
- Camera: side HOLD followed by a short YIELD so the strip has exit space.
- Secondary physical motion: one condensation trail across glass/reflection reads like a tear echo.
- Residue: empty rail + disturbed strip path; do not chase it.
- Beauty reason: warm pool light, stainless highlights and the single pale strip create a clean memory image rather than a prop demonstration.
- Avoid: readable promise text, rings, letters, second person, crying performance overload.

### P04｜Obsessive corridor
**Lyric responsibility:** `我为你 着了魔 发了疯 / 落得心事重重`

- First-frame scale: moving-medium-wide potential, deep locker corridor.
- K0 primary event: protagonist is already walking away from camera along a long repeated locker aisle; gait is established at frame 0.
- Action entrance: camera follows rear-three-quarter, accelerates along a real translational path, overtakes to side, then slightly leads from front-three-quarter.
- Camera grammar: `FOLLOW -> OVERTAKE -> LEAD`; never orbit in place.
- Subject budget: stable forward walk + one small gaze change only.
- Secondary motion: 1–2 hanging damp towels pass foreground and return; short occlusion only.
- Residue: after overtaking, the same repeated lockers still surround him; spatial escape failed.
- Beauty reason: repeating teal/ivory lockers and foreground fabric create controlled parallax and pressure.
- Avoid: running, spinning camera, multiple head turns, slamming lockers, extra people.

### P05｜His light goes out
**Lyric responsibility:** `你却要 失了魂 关了灯 / 和她情意浓浓`

- First-frame scale: architectural wide across the full pool.
- K0 primary event: protagonist stands small on one cool side of the pool under one still-lit neutral lamp; across the water, a far corner already contains two adjacent warm lamps above two close empty loungers.
- Action entrance: the lamp over/near protagonist switches off once; camera performs slow lateral DISCOVER until the paired warm lights/chairs become unmistakable.
- Character action: only a restrained look across the pool.
- Residue: his side stays dark; paired warm zone stays on.
- Beauty reason: huge black-blue water plane separates one cold figure from a tiny paired warm island.
- Avoid: silhouettes, shadow-couples, extra humans, lamp flicker, melodramatic pointing.

### P06｜Summer outside / winter inside / cover self
**Lyric responsibility:** `明明是盛夏时分 / 心却冷的像寒冬 / 亲手把当初真的自己葬送` + tail

- First-frame scale: full-body medium-wide in locker room with strong warm exterior visible through glass brick.
- K0 primary event: protagonist stands before a full-length mirror; a clean white bath towel is already held high with both hands at mirror top edge, beginning to cover only the uppermost strip of reflection.
- Action entrance: he lowers the towel slowly and continuously until the reflection is fully hidden.
- Camera: HOLD through the identity-extinction action, then a world-opening retreat down the locker aisle.
- Environment: outside remains visibly gold-hot summer; interior reads cold cyan and damp.
- Residue / clean tail: covered mirror, small still figure, summer light continuing without him changing it.
- Settled end: use 29.780–31.922s music tail for breathing room, not a new action.
- Beauty reason: warm/cold split is architectural and physical; the final white rectangle of the covered mirror becomes a strong negative-space image.
- Avoid: burial ground, coffin, grave, literal death, snow indoors, visible breath, supernatural freezing.

## 4. Camera-load budget

Hard operating target per source:
`1 primary camera relationship + 1 primary subject action + 1 secondary physical motion`.

- P01: mild push + peel + condensation.
- P02: overhead/short follow + turn/walk + ripple.
- P03: short yield + release fingers + strip/wind.
- P04: overtake tracking + stable walk + brief towel occlusion.
- P05: lateral discover + look + single light-off event.
- P06: hold/retreat + cover mirror + persistent warm/cold environment.

If a generated source spends control budget on an unintended extra action, remove that action rather than asking the model to perform everything harder.
