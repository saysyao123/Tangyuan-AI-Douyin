Generate **six separate independent 9:16 vertical cinematic still images**, never a collage, storyboard sheet or multi-panel image.

Same fictional East Asian adult male in all six frames: attractive but natural, late 20s to early 30s, short dark hair with slightly damp texture, restrained expression, realistic skin, no celebrity likeness. Same wardrobe throughout: off-white lightweight short-sleeve knit/thin summer shirt and charcoal loose trousers, progressively slightly damp but no costume change.

Same world: one modernist city indoor swimming club near closing time in midsummer. Pale aqua ceramic tile, brushed stainless steel, amber glass brick, wet concrete, clear pool water, warm hard summer daylight outside, increasingly cool restrained interior light. One controlled deep-red accent may come only from a pool lane rope. Cinematic photorealism, tactile wet materials, physically coherent light, fine natural grain, elegant composition. No beach, no seaside resort, no rain-after exterior, no branded sportswear, no readable signage/numbers, no text, logo or watermark, no extra people.

Each frame is a **performable 0-second K0 anchor**, not a completed poster pose.
