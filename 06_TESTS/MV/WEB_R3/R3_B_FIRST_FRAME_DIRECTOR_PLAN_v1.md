## Director spine
The 24s excerpt moves through eight conditional sentences. The visual arc should feel like one private question becoming increasingly concrete, then softening into release.

Recurring grammar:
- wind = unsaid language;
- rain = response;
- reflections = memory/uncertainty;
- warm domestic light = home;
- physical dissolve = healing;
- dawn = love becoming real rather than fairy tale.

Do not illustrate every noun literally. Each frame must have one dominant event and one clear dynamic entrance.

## S01｜0.000–3.000｜如果风会替我说话
Role: `HOOK`
Frame: extreme eye close-up, elongated almond eyes as first visual center; smoke-charcoal veil crosses lower face; a narrow gust catches one veil edge and several fine hair strands; cool window light with a thin warm rim.
Dynamic entrance: veil lifts diagonally, eye catchlight shifts, hair crosses frame.
Memory point: the wind visibly becomes the carrier of the unsaid sentence.

## S02｜3.000–6.000｜如果雨会替我回答
Role: `RESPONSE`
Frame: three-quarter close profile beside rain-covered glass; her real eye and its rain-broken reflection share the frame; dark room behind, one warm lamp far back.
Dynamic entrance: droplets merge and slide through the reflected eye; curtain breathes slightly.
Memory point: rain does not merely decorate the window; it visually answers the gaze.

## S03｜6.000–8.000｜如果我还会想起他
Role: `MEMORY`
Frame: medium-close, woman seated at the edge of a lived-in room; foreground eye remains sharp while an empty warm-lit doorway/chair is softly reflected in a dark mirror; no second person.
Dynamic entrance: she does not turn fully; only gaze shifts toward the empty warm area.
Memory point: absence is represented by occupied light, not by generating a male figure.

## S04｜8.000–12.000｜如果还能一起回家
Role: `HOME / HOLD`
Frame: wider interior-to-exterior composition; she stands near a rain-damp threshold or old apartment corridor, foreground cool, far end a single warm home light; veil and coat remain dark; one hand lightly touches the doorframe.
Dynamic entrance: warm light flickers into steadiness; wind from corridor moves veil inward.
Memory point: "home" is a reachable warm coordinate, not a literal couple scene.

## S05｜12.000–15.000｜如果梦能模糊真假
Role: `DREAM / AMBIGUITY`
Frame: symmetrical close-medium near a shallow water surface or dark mirror; same woman appears once physically and once only as a distorted reflection, veil identical; eye alignment nearly overlaps but not perfectly.
Dynamic entrance: a single ripple or breath of mist breaks the alignment.
Memory point: truth and dream are separated only by a thin unstable surface.

## S06｜15.000–18.000｜如果痛能随之融化
Role: `HEALING`
Frame: tactile close shot including eyes and hands; she holds a small clear ice/glass-like pendant or frost-coated object near warm lamplight; no wound, no blood; her eyes are calmer than S01.
Dynamic entrance: condensation/water runs down the object; warm light spreads through it.
Memory point: pain changes state rather than disappearing through melodrama.

## S07｜18.000–20.000｜如果我们还是傻瓜
Role: `TENDERNESS`
Frame: medium shot beside a windowsill with two simple paper wind objects / folded paper figures, one slightly crooked; she looks at them with softened eyes; veil remains complete.
Dynamic entrance: one paper object turns unexpectedly in a small gust while the other follows a beat later.
Memory point: "我们" is suggested by two imperfect objects, not a second human.

## S08｜20.000–24.000｜如果爱不只是童话
Role: `RELEASE / FINAL PEAK`
Frame: dawn exterior or rooftop garden after rain; medium close-to-wide balance; the same woman faces the first warm light, city/trees softened behind her; charcoal veil catches backlight and becomes luminous at the edges without becoming transparent enough to expose the lower face.
Dynamic entrance: wind opens the veil outward, hair and fabric move into light; eyes finally hold steady rather than searching.
Memory point: the final image transforms "童话" from fantasy spectacle into a real morning that can be touched.

## Set-level camera differentiation
- S01 extreme close frontal/oblique

## Hard continuity
- same fictional woman across all frames;
- smoke-charcoal semi-transparent veil always fully covers lower face;
- no second real human;
- no readable text / logo / advertising;
- no gratuitous fantasy effects;
- low-frequency skin + high-frequency eyes/hair/veil details;
- beauty must remain dynamically executable in 5s source generation.
